# TOEFL Speaking

<br>

---

<br>

每週至少 3 次，用 ChatGPT / Grok 語音模式做有主題的對談。新制 Speaking 只有 ~8 分鐘：**Listen and Repeat**（聽句複述，7 題）+ **Take an Interview**（訪談問答，4 題，每題只有 45 秒）。所以練習重點是：**45 秒內講出有結構的答案**，不是長篇大論。

<br>

## 練習方式

### 1. Interview 模擬（主力）

把下面的 prompt 貼給 ChatGPT / Grok 語音模式，讓它扮演考官：

```
You are a TOEFL Speaking examiner conducting the "Take an Interview" task.
Ask me one question at a time about the topic: {TOPIC}.
Give me 45 seconds to answer, then:
1. Point out grammar or word-choice mistakes I made
2. Show me a better model answer in 2-3 sentences
3. Ask a follow-up question on the same topic
Keep the conversation going for 4 questions total, then give me a band score (1-6) with reasons.
```

### 2. Listen and Repeat 自主練

拿 [Listening](../listening/README.md) 的精聽材料，逐句暫停 → 憑記憶複述整句 → 對照原文。每次 10 句。

<br>

## 主題清單（每次挑一個，練完打勾）

### Campus / 學習

- [ ] Why do you want to pursue a master's degree online?
- [ ] Describe your favorite way to learn something new
- [ ] Do you prefer studying alone or in groups?
- [ ] Describe a teacher who influenced you
- [ ] Should universities require attendance?

### Work / 科技（貼近本業，順便練面試英文）

- [ ] Describe a challenging project you worked on
- [ ] How has AI changed the way you work?
- [ ] Do you prefer working from home or in an office?
- [ ] What makes a good software engineer?
- [ ] Describe a time you had to learn a technology quickly

### 日常 / 觀點

- [ ] Describe your hometown
- [ ] What do you do to stay healthy?
- [ ] Is it better to save money or enjoy it now?
- [ ] Describe a book or movie that impressed you
- [ ] Should children learn programming at school?

> 主題練完了就叫 Claude 再生一批：`toefl: more speaking topics`

<br>

## 45 秒答題模板

```
Opinion  → 直接表態一句
Reason 1 → because... + 一個具體例子
Reason 2 → also... （時間夠才講）
Wrap     → so that's why...
```
